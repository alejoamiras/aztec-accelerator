# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and
healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that supports the community include:

- showing empathy, patience, and respect for different viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive, technically specific feedback;
- taking responsibility, apologizing when appropriate, and learning from mistakes;
- focusing on what is best for users and the community; and
- respecting privacy and avoiding the disclosure of another person's sensitive information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults or derogatory comments, sexualized attention,
trolling, sustained disruption, threats, stalking, and publishing another person's private
information without permission.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, project communications, and public
spaces where someone is representing the project or its community.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to
[alejo@aztec.foundation](mailto:alejo@aztec.foundation) with the subject
`[aztec-accelerator conduct]`. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and impartially as practical.
The identity of a reporter will be shared only when necessary to investigate or respond, subject to
legal obligations.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict, or
permanently ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
Enforcement will consider context, severity, repetition, intent, and impact. Retaliation against a
reporter or participant in an investigation is prohibited.
